# Personal contribution

This project was developed as a group project, so many parts of the work were discussed and adjusted together. My contribution was mainly in shaping the project direction, setting up the modelling framework, running part of the experiments, and writing the sections connected to my work.

At the beginning, I contributed to the project idea and helped turn it into a concrete experimental question: whether it is better to train on all available noisy data or on a smaller cleaned dataset for Haitian Creole--English machine translation.

For the experimental setup, the model and training process were developed collaboratively by the team. My part was to set up the overall training framework and build the main fairseq Transformer pipeline, which we then used across the different data-cleaning conditions.

I also trained two of the experimental models and helped keep track of the workflow so that the different conditions were easier to compare. After the experiments were completed, I summarised part of the results and carried out an error analysis on 50 translation examples.

For the written report, I wrote the parts related to my work, including the project motivation, the experimental setup, the relevant results summary, and the error analysis.
